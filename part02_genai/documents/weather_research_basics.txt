Zimbabwe Weather Research: Demonstration Knowledge Document

Weather describes short-term atmospheric conditions at a particular place
and time. Examples include temperature, rainfall, humidity, cloud cover
and wind speed.

Climate describes long-term patterns in weather, commonly assessed using
observations over periods of 30 years or more.

Rainfall and temperature patterns in Zimbabwe vary by season, location,
elevation and year. A general description of the climate cannot establish
the current weather conditions in a particular town.

Temperature prediction models estimate a target variable using patterns
learned from historical data. A one-hour-ahead temperature prediction
should be reported as a model estimate, not as a direct observation or
a guarantee of future conditions.

Reliable weather research should distinguish observed data, model
predictions, scientific background information and uncertainty.
